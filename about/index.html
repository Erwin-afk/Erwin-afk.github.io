About

I build software that ships and records that move.

I am Nyisztor Ervin, a developer and music producer from Hungary. Two disciplines that turn out to want the same things: structure, restraint, and something that actually lands.

On the software side I build my own mobile and web products end to end, mostly for Hungarian markets and their very specific rules. JogsiGo runs driving schools, Trippo keeps trip logs for drivers, Cerka gives students a better school app, and TakiGo is a home-cleaning marketplace for Budapest.

Before that it was mostly Python: Discord bots, tools, and things I built to find out how they worked.

On the music side I make Mixxx and beats as Fenzi, three years in, releasing on Spotify and YouTube.

I don't take client work. Everything on this site is my own.

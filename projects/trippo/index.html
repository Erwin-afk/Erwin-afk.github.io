Trippo

Automatic trip log for Hungarian drivers. You drive, you swipe, the paperwork is done.

Trippo records every drive in the background. Swipe right for business, left for private, and at the end of the month it produces the trip log and travel order for your accountant, calculated from the official NAV rates.

The problem

If you use your own car for business in Hungary, the company can reimburse you tax-free. In exchange you need a trip log (útnyilvántartás) and a travel order (kiküldetési rendelvény) every month.

Most people rebuild both from memory at the end of the month, in Excel. It is easy to skip a trip or mistype a kilometre, and a forgotten trip is money lost.

The target for Trippo was simple: a full month closed in under three minutes.

Three steps, no notebook

  1. 1

    Drive

    There is nothing to start. Trippo records the route, kilometres and addresses in the background.

  2. 2

    Swipe

    Right for business, left for private, up for commuting. Recurring trips get classified on their own over time.

  3. 3

    Done

    Close the month and the PDF and Excel go to your accountant in one tap.

The maths behind a swipe

Every trip shows what it is worth before you swipe. The reimbursement is fuel, at the official consumption norm and the monthly NAV price, plus 15 Ft/km for car use.

Example with a sample fuel price. NAV publishes the real price every month.

The norms come from government decree 60/1992, and the monthly fuel prices are fetched by a scheduled function. The engine itself is pure TypeScript with integer money maths, so it is fully testable without a device and runs just as well on a server.

What is in it

Automatic classification

Clients, home and work, calendar events and learned habits decide the obvious trips for you.

Map and planner

A live trip, the month's routes, and destination planning that hands off to Waze, Google Maps or Apple Maps.

Trip details

Route, time, speed and stops, with splitting and merging for the days that went differently.

Odometer reconciliation

Start- and end-of-year readings, checked against the kilometres recorded.

Locked months

Closed months carry a verification code and a change log.

Reading from photos

Odometers and fuel receipts are read from a photo, using Claude.

Cars and imports

Own cars, company cars and vans under 3.5 t, fuel receipts, and Google Timeline import.

Hands-free start

Start with CarPlay, Siri or a widget, with live kilometres on the lock screen and Apple Watch.

Private trips stay private

A trip log is a record of where you go every day, so the privacy model came before the features.

Private routes stay on the phone

The route, addresses and stops of a private trip never reach the server. Only the kilometres and the time do.

EU only

Data is stored on EU servers, and road snapping is built on a self-hosted OSRM server, so GPS traces never go to a third-party maps API.

No location in Live Activities

The server side of the Live Activity receives the text shown on screen, and never a location.

Yours to delete

The account can be deleted at any time, from the app or from the site.

The hard parts

Background tracking that stays small

The background task only buffers GPS points. Trips are segmented when the app opens, which keeps background work tiny and the logic unit-testable.

Live Activities without the app

iOS cannot start an activity from the background, or run anything once the car has stopped. So push-to-start and a scheduled server sweep do both.

One report, three runtimes

The same workbook code builds the Excel on the phone, in Edge Functions and in the browser tools on trippo.hu, and a test fails if the copies drift.

Stack

Expo SDK 57 with Expo Router, Reanimated and gesture-handler for the swipe deck, Supabase in the EU, and a custom native Swift module.

A free portal for accountants

Accountants are the people who ask for these records, so they get a free web portal: every client's closed months in one place, who has closed last month, and the Excel one click away. trippo.hu also carries a free toolkit with a kilometre calculator, a lease-VAT calculator, an Excel template and guides.

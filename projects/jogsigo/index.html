The problem

Most Hungarian driving schools still run on phone calls, Messenger and a notebook. Students cannot see free slots, so every booking is a conversation. A cancelled lesson is usually lost income, because nobody on the waiting list hears that the slot opened. Working out who has paid means searching a bank account by hand.

On top of that the trade is regulated: minimum lessons and kilometres per licence category, daily limits for students, exam deadlines, and KÖKÉNY, the system where lessons have to be announced and recorded.

JogsiGo replaces all of that with one system that the three people involved work in together.

One system, three roles

Students

Book from the instructor's free slots, follow progress against the legal minimums, and pay, all in one place.

  • Book and cancel free of charge until the school's deadline
  • Waitlist and earlier-slot alerts with a free swap
  • Progress per category, with the hours left until the exam
  • Weekly series booking and a meeting point per lesson
  • Bank transfer, online payment or lesson packages
  • Chat, document uploads and an exam-day guide

Instructors

The whole day on one screen, and closing a lesson takes seconds.

  • Today's lessons, students and meeting points
  • Close a lesson with kilometres, errors and notes
  • Alerts for deadlines, exam-ready students and students who stopped booking
  • On a sick day, the lessons move to free colleagues in one step
  • Works without signal and syncs later
  • A personal calendar feed

The office

A browser dashboard for the day, the students, the money and the paperwork.

  • Day overview, student progress and outstanding balances
  • Excel import, including lessons already completed
  • Payment links and bank-statement matching
  • Exam-ready students, deadlines and a printable training log
  • Automatic fees for late cancellations and no-shows
  • Instructor pay, monthly statements and statistics
  • Branches, office staff and ownership transfer

The law is in the code

The rules of decree 24/2005 (GKM) are part of the product, not a PDF in a drawer. The app knows what a student may book, and the school does not have to police it.

Every licence category

Courses with their minimum lessons and kilometres, with prices set per category.

Daily limits

At most 4 lessons a day, 2 in the basic stage, and a break after two in a row.

Night lessons

Counted from the calculated sunset for that day, not from a fixed time.

Exam deadlines

The 9- and 12-month deadlines and KRESZ exam validity, with alerts in time to act.

KÖKÉNY assistant

Lists what to announce, record or delete, in KÖKÉNY order, with a copy button.

Paperwork checklist

Medical certificate expiry per student, and guardian consent for minors by e-mail link.

Payments and invoicing

The part most school software skips. Money and invoices follow Hungarian practice, including the awkward cases.

Money goes straight to the school

Stripe Connect, SimplePay and Barion, each on the school's own account, with no separate JogsiGo fee.

Bank transfers without detective work

Payment request links, and automatic matching from the bank statement.

Every payment can be invoiced

Online, cash or transfer, through the school's own Számlázz.hu account.

Company payers

A numbered proforma from Számlázz.hu, then the company's invoice once the transfer is confirmed.

The awkward cases

Refunds, chargebacks, storno invoices, fee corrections and discounted packages.

Keys out of reach

Each school's Számla Agent key is encrypted in Supabase Vault, and payment secrets only exist server-side.

How it is built

One Expo codebase serves iOS, Android and the office web app, over a Postgres database that enforces the rules itself.

Writes go through the database

Row-level security everywhere, and every write path is a security-definer function, so a modified client cannot skip a rule.

Offline by design

The last loaded data stays on the device, and lessons closed without a connection are sent when it returns.

Fast for large schools

A 120-day lesson window loads page by page past the API's 1,000-row cap, older lessons arrive summed per student, and Realtime patches single rows.

Load tested

A browser test seeds 1,200 students and 6,000 lessons and checks the load times.

Scheduled work in Postgres

A daily pg_cron job sends the evening-before reminders and paperwork warnings, and its last runs show up in the admin view.

Three layers of tests

Database tests on PGlite, Edge Function tests on Deno, and Puppeteer browser tests that run nightly against a local Supabase stack.

Monitoring

A heartbeat checks the web app, database, functions and daily job every 3 hours. App errors carry their version and OTA update id, and stack traces map back to source.

A help chat that knows the product

The office has a built-in help chat, running on Claude through an Edge Function.

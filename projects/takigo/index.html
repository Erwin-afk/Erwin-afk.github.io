Overview

TakiGo started as CleanValet. It is the largest of these four codebases: one Expo client that serves three different roles over a single Supabase backend, with phone authentication, row-level security and Stripe payments.

A marketplace has to earn trust from both sides before anyone pays, so most of the work went into what happens around a booking rather than the booking itself.

Three roles, one app

Homeowners

Choose what they need and see the final price straight away.

  • A fixed price up front, not an estimate
  • Messaging with the cleaner in the app
  • Live tracking while the cleaner is on the way
  • Recurring cleans with no service fee on repeat bookings

Cleaners

Set their own schedule and follow their earnings.

  • A live feed of nearby jobs
  • Calendar sync over iCal
  • Payouts to their bank account through Stripe

Admins

Keep both sides of the marketplace honest.

  • Approve cleaners, with identity and business status verified
  • Oversee bookings, payouts and insurance claims
  • Broadcast and direct messages to users

Money and trust

Hold first, capture later

Booking only places a hold on the card. The money moves when the work is confirmed.

Damage protection

A claim can be filed from the booking itself, with photos and video.

Payout integrity

Stripe Connect onboarding, automatic payouts and instant payouts, with database checks before a job can be accepted.

Invoices

Invoices generated through Számlázz.hu, with refunds and tips handled in dedicated functions.

JogsiGo

Driving-school software for Hungary: booking, payments and paperwork in one system.

Students book lessons in the app, instructors run their day from their phone, and the office manages everything from a web dashboard. Online payments go straight to the school, every payment can be invoiced, and the legal rules for every licence category are built in.

Trippo

Automatic trip log for Hungarian drivers. You drive, you swipe, the paperwork is done.

Trippo records every drive in the background. Swipe right for business, left for private, and at the end of the month it produces the trip log and travel order for your accountant, calculated from the official NAV rates.

Cerka

A redesigned, unofficial e-KRÉTA client for students in Hungary.

Cerka puts a student's school day in one clean app: a live lesson countdown, grades with targets, absences against the legal limits, and widgets for the lock screen. It has no server, so school data only travels between the phone and the school system.

TakiGo

A home-cleaning marketplace for Budapest, with vetted cleaners and fixed prices.

Homeowners book and pay, cleaners pick up jobs from a live feed, and admins approve cleaners and payouts, all in one app. The card is only held at booking, and the money moves when the homeowner confirms the work is done.

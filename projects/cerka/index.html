Cerka

A redesigned, unofficial e-KRÉTA client for students in Hungary.

Cerka puts a student's school day in one clean app: a live lesson countdown, grades with targets, absences against the legal limits, and widgets for the lock screen. It has no server, so school data only travels between the phone and the school system.

What it does

Live countdown

How long is left of the lesson or the break, in the app, on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Timetable

A swipeable week and a month calendar, with breaks, free periods, cancellations and holidays.

Grades

Average, trend and class average per subject, with a warning when an average sits on a rounding edge.

What do I need?

Works out how many good grades get you to a target, with a what-if calculator.

Absences

Tracks the 250-hour and 30%-per-subject limits, with a planner for working and evening students.

The small things

A morning briefing, a bag checklist with an evening reminder, a half-year recap and badges.

Inside the app

Demo mode, with a made-up student.

Private because there is no server

School records belong to the student, so the cleanest design was to never hold them at all.

The password is never seen

Sign-in happens on the school system's own login page in a WebView, using OAuth2 with PKCE. The app only catches the redirect.

Tokens in the secure store

Only the tokens are kept, in the phone's encrypted storage, with support for several student accounts.

Nothing to collect

No backend, no analytics, no ads and no crash service. In Apple's terms: Data Not Collected.

Works offline

A persisted cache keeps the app useful without signal, and a background task finds new items for notifications.

Unofficial

Cerka is an independent app. It is not an official product and is not affiliated with eKRÉTA Informatikai Zrt.
